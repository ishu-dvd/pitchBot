# A buyer who starts in English and moves to Hindi halfway through, **without ever saying
# they are going to** - which on an Indian B2B call is the ordinary case, not the edge one.
# Nobody announces a language change; they just make it. Before this was handled the agent
# carried on in English for the rest of the conversation, and on the voice path the
# transcriber stayed forced to English too, so the Hindi turns came back as confident
# English nobody said.
#
# Run: pitchbot-talk --script examples/switch-en-hi.txt
#
# Turn 1 opens in the declared language. Nothing to detect.
We run an online toy store.

# Turn 2 is the first Hindi turn. The agent does NOT switch here, on purpose: one turn is
# not evidence, and switching on it would let a single borrowed word change the reply
# language, the voice and the transcriber all at once.
हमें एक कैटलॉग और ऑनलाइन पेमेंट चाहिए।

# Turn 3 is the second consecutive Hindi turn, so the switch happens now and is
# acknowledged in Hindi before anything else is said.
यह हमारे लिए थोड़ा महंगा लग रहा है।

# Turn 4 stays in Hindi. The objection above was answered in Hindi; this continues.
हमारा बजट लगभग 150000 रुपये है।

# Turn 5 agrees, in Hindi. The agent closes rather than asking one more question.
ठीक है, शुरू करें।

# Turns 6 and 7 go back to English, again without saying so. Switching has to work in
# both directions: a conversation that could move into Hindi but never move back would
