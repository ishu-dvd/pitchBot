# Turn 2 fills features. Ordinary qualification, no stance.

# Turn 3 is a price objection. Before this existed the agent answered it with the next

# Turn 4 is a competitor. Different concern, different answer.
We are also comparing another vendor.

# Turn 5 is a stall, the most common close-killer of the three.

# Turn 6 fills budget now that the concerns have been answered.
Our budget is around 150000 rupees.
